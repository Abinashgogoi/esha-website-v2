ESHA Hub: Advancing Research, Innovation and Community Development

ESHA Hub is a registered nonprofit society based in Assam, working across research, innovation, technology, community development and practical problem-solving.

We bring together researchers, creators, technical contributors and community members to explore ideas, develop projects and support socially relevant initiatives through a collaborative and learning-oriented approach.

Hub FAQs

ESHA Hub connects bright minds with the resources they need to transform ideas into reality, fostering a collaborative ecosystem of innovation.

We champion ethical and forward-thinking advancements in AI, healthtech, space, and other cutting-edge domains, ensuring a better tomorrow.

ESHA Hub currently has ten permanent organisational members. Seven members constitute its Governing Body, while three permanent functional officers form the Organisational Secretariat. The Secretariat supports science education and mentorship, pharmacy and community-health activities, and field operations and logistics.

Our Guiding Principles

We support responsible, collaborative and practical work according to the purpose, readiness and available resources of each initiative.

Scientific Rigor

We encourage documented methods, transparent assumptions, responsible experimentation and appropriate review according to the stage and nature of each project.

Inclusive Innovation

Technology must serve everyone. We prioritize low-cost, scalable solutions that address challenges in developing regions and local communities.

Mentorship First

We encourage experienced contributors to support learners and emerging project members through practical guidance, shared discussion and project-based exposure whenever appropriate and available.
