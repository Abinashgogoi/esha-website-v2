Donation Policy and Transparency

What donation does and does not mean

A donation to ESHA Hub is a voluntary contribution to nonprofit work. It is not a purchase, service fee, membership fee or project-entry fee.

  • Donation does not guarantee mentorship, membership, internship, employment or project access.
  • Donation does not provide commercial rights, intellectual property rights or decision-making rights.
  • Funds are used according to organisational priorities, approved activities and compliance needs.
  • ESHA Hub may request donor details for records, payment verification, receipts or legal compliance.

Tax and Legal Notice

Important donor notice

ESHA Hub has received provisional registration and provisional approval under Form 106. Eligible Indian donors may receive donation receipts as per applicable Income Tax rules after successful payment confirmation and required donor details.

Foreign contributions are not accepted unless ESHA Hub has the required legal approval. Please do not donate from foreign bank accounts, foreign cards, or on behalf of foreign sources unless ESHA Hub officially confirms acceptance.

ESHA Hub currently accepts donations only from eligible Indian donors through approved Indian payment channels.

FAQ

Donation questions

No. Donation is voluntary and separate from joining, access or participation decisions.

Will donation guarantee mentorship, membership, internship or project access?

No. Donation does not guarantee mentorship, membership, internship, employment, project access or decision-making rights.
